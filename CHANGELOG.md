# Changelog

Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com). Reference F-, D-, AV-, BUG- and IMP- IDs for traceability. Upstream Descent 3 changes arrive via merges and are summarised, not re-listed; see upstream's CHANGELOG for detail.

## [Unreleased]
### Added (Phase 4)
- Weapon sidecar (D-007): new weapons come from `data/tartarus/weapons.txt`, each a copy of a stock weapon with its own name and changes, in free slots after the stock ones; built into the game, or read from `weapons.txt` in the writable folder for tuning; `weaponinfo` lists them. The first entry is GravMissile (F-021), not yet fireable — 2026-11-15.
- Cloak on demand (F-020): C turns invisible for up to 8 s, for 30 energy and 8 a second, dropping when energy falls to 20 (`cloak_floor`) rather than draining you to 0; firing, bumping a robot or a hit of 10 or more breaks it into a 6 s cooldown, otherwise 3 s; a gauge under the reticle shows cloaked, recharging and ready. Robots already lose a cloaked player in stock — 2026-11-15.
- Energy ↔ shield converter (F-030): hold B to turn energy into shields, N for the reverse, at half value (`conv_rate` 25/s, `conv_ratio` 0.5, never below `conv_floor` 20); refuelling sound, CONVERT on the flight HUD, a message when it stops — 2026-11-15.
- Save and load without Alt+F2 (BUG-011): the options deck (F2) opens on a Game page with Save game and Load game, and the console has `savegame` and `loadgame`, which can be bound; for desktops such as Cinnamon that take Alt+F2 for themselves — 2026-11-15.
- Guide-Bot commands (F-072): Next key, Door I can now open and Unexplored area on the deck Guide-Bot menu, and `guide key|door|area` on the console (bindable). Tartarus picks the nearest target by route, with doors you cannot open counted as walls, and the Guide-Bot flies there as for its own Find commands; a key with no way to it yet is reported as such — 2026-11-15.
- Guide-Bot route overlay (F-072): `r_showpath` draws the Guide-Bot's planned route (same HOURGLASS cheat as the other overlays), and `pathdump` lists it to the console and the log; blocked legs show red, and `roominfo` lists a room's openings — 2026-11-14.
- Debug overlays (F-013): `r_showaxes` (world axes at the ship), `r_showobjinfo` (labels on nearby objects: type, name, number, handle, distance) and `r_showcollide` (collision spheres). Console cheats unlocked by HOURGLASS; they mark the pilot a cheater and are refused in Classic Mode and multiplayer — 2026-11-14.
### Added (Phase 2)
- Keys screen (F1, F-069): after the fixed keys it lists the pilot's own bindings for dropping mines and other countermeasures, inventory, flare, headlight, rear view and automap, read live from the controls, so a changed binding shows as changed — 2026-11-15.
- Deck end-of-level review and Adjust settings screens (F-069, D-016): the Pilot Performance Review after each level, and the keyboard and joystick-mouse settings behind Adjust settings, now use the deck in Tartarus mode, and the deck's pilot page has Copy controls; Classic Mode and multiplayer keep the stock screens, and the multiplayer customisation screens (ship, logo, taunts, pilot picture) stay stock. The deck's force gain setting goes from 0 to 100% (the stock slider sets full gain at almost every position, BUG-014) — 2026-11-07.
### Changed (Phase 2)
- Virtual-joystick mouse (F-067): full-turn travel is now `m_vj_travel`, in mouse counts, so the stick feels the same at any window size; it replaces `m_vj_radius`, a share of the window height. New defaults are the owner's settings, as the old ones flew badly: auto-centring on, centre speed 2.0, speed 0.75 and inverted pitch (mouse forward climbs) — 2026-11-07.
### Added (Phase 3)
- Automap symbols and filters (F-050): keys, doors (red when locked), goals and powerups show where you have scanned, each switchable with F5 to F8 on the map or `map_show_*`; powerups are off by default. Markers are thin symbols and terrain is a wire grid. 4 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Automap compass and closing cue (F-050, F-051): a heading tape on the map, and the roll arc and heading tape stand out for a moment after closing it; `map_reanchor_time`. 2 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Automap: whatever the scanner has not reached is now blank, including openings to unknown rooms (F-050); `map_scan_dim` above 0 brings back dim edges — 2026-11-05.
- Full-resolution automap (F-050): in Tartarus mode the map opens in the deck casing at your resolution instead of inside TelCom, with sharp lines and an amber ship marker in place of the stock model. It shows the level name and the chosen marker's message. Classic Mode and multiplayer keep TelCom — 2026-11-05.
- Automap scans survive save and load (F-050), in a `.amap` file beside each save; the save format is unchanged and older saves simply start unscanned. 6 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Automap scanner look (F-050): a pale green wireframe on black in place of the shaded rooms, dim edges for surfaces seen but not scanned, and new surfaces flash when the map opens. `map_scan_flash`. 2 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Automap laser scanner, prototype (F-050): the ship scans surfaces within 200 units as it flies, and the map shows scanned surfaces brightly and others dimly. `map_scan`, `map_scan_range`, `map_scan_rays`, `map_scan_dim`, `map_scan_status`. Scans are not saved yet. 8 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Flight HUD, fourth stage (F-046): status words either side of the heading tape, shown only while active: SLIDE, BANK, HOLD, SNAP, BOOST, LIGHT; `hud_flight_status`. 3 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Hold and snap to upright (F-043): hold X to roll upright, tap Z to roll to the nearest upright, and `al_strength` for extra auto-level (off by default). Any roll input cancels. Keys and speeds are settings; speeds and strength are on the deck's Gameplay page. Stock physics is unchanged. 7 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Attitude ball and gizmo opacity (F-041, F-042): `hud_attitude_alpha` and `hud_gizmo_alpha`, also on the deck's Gameplay page — 2026-11-05.
- Reticle styles (F-047): in Tartarus mode a drawn reticle replaces the old bitmaps, with a choice of Gunsight, Brackets, Chevrons, Cross or Stock on the deck's Gameplay page. Bars and pips show which guns and missile launchers are ready, as before. Classic Mode keeps the stock reticle. 5 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Flight HUD, third stage (F-046): speed tape on the left, range to the wall ahead on the right, climb rate beneath it; `hud_flight_speed`, `hud_flight_range*`, `hud_flight_climb`, `hud_flight_tape_*`. 4 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Flight HUD, second stage (F-046, F-045): a compass heading tape along the top of the canopy and a roll arc; `hud_flight_heading*`, `hud_flight_roll*`. The ladder stays in the middle of the view. All orientation aids now draw in batches, one call per colour, through the new `rend_DrawTriangles2D`. 5 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Flight HUD on the canopy, first stage (F-046, D-017): in cockpit view, a pale green horizon, pitch ladder and flight path marker that line up with the real world and sit behind the cockpit frame. On by default in Tartarus mode; `hud_flight*`. `hud_aids` turns off every orientation aid, and the up-tick now defaults to off. 16 unit tests — 2026-11-05.
- Attitude ball (F-041) and axis gizmo (F-042): off by default (D-010), with size and position settings; `hud_attitude*`, `hud_gizmo*`, also on the deck's Gameplay page. All three orientation aids together cost about 0.05 ms a frame. 7 unit tests — 2026-11-04.
- Reticle up-tick (F-040, D-010): a wedge at the reticle that points to world up, fading out when you are upright. On by default in Tartarus mode; `hud_uptick`, `hud_uptick_len`, `hud_uptick_fade_deg`, also on the deck's Gameplay page. 9 unit tests — 2026-11-04.
### Added (Phase 2)
- Deck menus (F-069, D-016): the main menu, Save, Load (with the save's screenshot, BUG-013), New Game, level warp, Pilots, keyboard, joystick and mouse bindings, weapon order, the Keys help, the Guide-Bot menu and all message and text-entry boxes now use the deck in Tartarus mode, at the chosen resolution with the art scaled to fill the screen, with keyboard, pointer and stock hotkeys. A Key bindings row on the Controls page opens the bindings from F2 and the main menu. Classic Mode and multiplayer keep the stock screens — 2026-11-04.
- Options deck (F-069, D-016): a new cassette-futurism options screen on F2 and from the main menu in Tartarus mode, amber on gunmetal, with Graphics, Controls, Audio, Gameplay and Tartarus pages generated from the settings, resolution and fullscreen, in-menu scanline, phosphor and casing settings, keyboard and mouse input, and a route to the stock options. Classic Mode keeps the stock menu. 7 unit tests. Test tools `tools/gamedrive.py` and `tools/run_probe.sh` — 2026-11-03.
- Graphics options (F-068): VSync now works (it was never applied, BUG-010) with an adaptive mode; frame-rate cap `r_maxfps` (default 60 as before); trilinear and anisotropic filtering (on and 4x by default); 24-bit depth buffer (less flicker on distant surfaces); MSAA anti-aliasing `r_msaa` (off by default); render scale `r_renderscale` (supersampling above 1, faster below 1); `r_gamma`; `r_restart` applies frame changes without restarting. Console settings for now; a Graphics page comes with F-069 — 2026-11-02.
- Raw mouse (F-062): `m_raw` reads the mouse every frame instead of upstream's 20 times a second (off by default for the stock mouse until its sensitivity is tuned; always on for the virtual joystick, which now responds up to 50 ms sooner), `m_sensitivity`, `m_accel`. Console command `where` prints the ship's position and orientation. 4 unit tests — 2026-11-02.
- Options (F2): the Mouse tab is now the **Tartarus** tab and adds a rear-view mirror choice for the left small window: Stock, Top left or Top right (wide mirror), plus "Custom" when set from the console (F-060) — 2026-11-02.
- Small-view windows (rear view, Guide-Bot, missile camera) in the HUD layout: size, shape and position per window with `hud_sv_left_*` / `hud_sv_right_*` and `hud_sv_bigger_scale`; the defaults reproduce the stock layout exactly (F-060). 6 unit tests — 2026-11-01.
- Command-line `-skipbriefing` (no pre-level briefing or ship selection) and `-skipcinematics` (in-game cinematics such as level intros end as soon as a key could skip them; the ship is placed where the cinematic would leave it). For quicker testing: level 1 is flyable in about 9 s instead of 21 s — 2026-11-01.
- Menus no longer sit between black bars on widescreen displays: the bars show a blurred, darkened copy of the screen (`r_bar_fill`, `r_bar_fill_brightness`) (F-060) — 2026-11-01.
- Field of view 60–120° in Tartarus mode (stock 72–90, kept in Classic Mode): Options → Video slider and `r_fov` in the console (F-060) — 2026-11-01.
- Virtual-joystick mouse (F-067, D-013): `m_mode 1` (default in Tartarus mode) treats the mouse offset from centre as a turn rate, so the ship keeps turning until the mouse comes back; `m_mode 2` auto-centres. Dead zone, radius, curve, sensitivity, invert and roll options (`m_vj_*`), an on-screen indicator, recentre on the middle mouse button or `m_recentre`, and resets on every context change (AV-013). Classic Mode keeps the stock mouse. 10 unit tests — 2026-11-01.
- Options (F2) → Mouse tab: mouse mode, speed, centre speed (auto-centre mode), invert pitch, indicator and roll, saved to `tartarus.cfg`; the first Tartarus options page (F-067, F-069). The middle mouse button now only recentres the virtual joystick; F still fires flares — 2026-11-01.

### Fixed
- Walls turning to garbage colours after explosions on NVIDIA: the vertex stream overwrote polygons the GPU had not drawn yet; it now sends only the vertices used and writes them safely (BUG-012, upstream) — 2026-11-04.
- Right and middle mouse buttons were swapped on Linux: the middle button fired missiles. Right now fires secondaries and the middle button recentres the virtual joystick, as intended (BUG-007, upstream) — 2026-11-01.
- Mouse pointer far too fast on TelCom screens (briefings, ship select, objectives); it now moves as in the menus (BUG-006, upstream) — 2026-11-01.
- Screen resolution now restored by width x height instead of a list index that can point at a different size (BUG-004, upstream); `-windowed`/`-fullscreen` no longer overwrite the saved fullscreen choice (BUG-005, upstream) — 2026-11-01.
- Docs: removed `-setdir` from all run instructions; upstream dropped it in 2024 and the writable directory is SDL's preference path (BUG-001) — 2026-11-01.
- `tools/upstream-sync.sh`: trial-merges `upstream/main` in a scratch worktree and fails on conflicts outside `Descent3/tartarus/` and the ARCHITECTURE.md hook-point list (IMP-002, F-001, AV-002) — 2026-11-01.
